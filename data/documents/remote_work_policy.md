# Flexible and Hybrid Work Arrangement Policy

## Section 1: Hybrid Work Schedule and Office Days
Acme Tech operates on a hybrid model balancing in-person collaboration with remote flexibility.
- Employees residing within 30 miles of an Acme Tech hub office (San Francisco, Austin, New York) must work on-site at least 2 days per week.
- Designated company-wide anchor collaboration days are Tuesday and Thursday.
- Fully remote status requires approval from the Department VP and People Operations.

## Section 2: Home Office Setup Allowance
To maintain ergonomic comfort and productive workspaces:
- Eligible full-time hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time Home Office Setup Stipend of $750, refreshable once every 24 months.
- Approved items include ergonomic office chairs, standing desks, external monitors, keyboards, mice, and webcams.
- Acme Tech IT provides a standard dual-monitor dock and laptop stand upon request during onboarding.

## Section 3: High-Speed Internet Subsidy
- Remote employees and hybrid staff can expense an internet subsidy of $50 per month through Expensify.
- The minimum recommended internet connection speed is 100 Mbps download / 20 Mbps upload.

## Section 4: Work from Anywhere (WFA) Policy
- Employees in good standing may work remotely from an alternate domestic or international location for up to 30 business days per calendar year.
- International remote work requires at least 3 weeks advance notice to People Ops to evaluate international tax and labor implications.
