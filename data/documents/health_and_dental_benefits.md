# Acme Tech Health, Dental, and Wellness Benefits Guide

## Section 1: Health Insurance Plans
Acme Tech provides comprehensive medical coverage through BlueCross BlueShield under the corporate plan name "AcmeCare Gold".
- Coverage begins on the first day of the calendar month following the date of hire.
- In-network primary care physician copay is $20 per visit.
- Specialist consultation copay is $40 per visit.
- Emergency room visits have a $150 deductible, waived if admitted directly to the hospital.
- Prescription drug coverage tier 1 (generic) copay is $10; tier 2 (preferred brand) copay is $30; tier 3 (specialty) is 20% coinsurance.

## Section 2: Dental and Vision Coverage
- Dental coverage is administered through Delta Dental Premier.
- The annual dental maximum benefit is $1,500 per covered individual per plan year.
- Preventive care (two cleanings and one set of x-rays per year) is covered at 100% with no deductible.
- Basic procedures (cavity fillings, root canals) are covered at 80% after an annual $50 deductible.
- Vision coverage is administered via VSP Vision Care, providing one free comprehensive annual eye exam and a $200 annual frame/lens allowance.

## Section 3: Mental Health and Wellness Programs
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Provides up to 8 free confidential counseling sessions per topic per year through Spring Health.
- Gym and Fitness Reimbursement: Employees can claim up to $60 per month for gym memberships, fitness class subscriptions, or yoga studios.
- Headspace or Calm subscription is provided free of charge through the corporate benefits portal.
