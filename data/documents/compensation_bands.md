# Confidential Engineering & Management Compensation Bands (FY2026)

> STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL: This document is intended solely for People Managers, Directors, and Executive Leadership. Unauthorized disclosure, copying, or dissemination to non-managerial staff is strictly prohibited.

## Section 1: Engineering Management Pay Bands (US Tech Hubs)
Base salary ranges are benchmarked annually against Radford P75 market data for San Francisco and New York.
- L5 (Engineering Team Lead): Base $160,000 - $190,000 | Target Bonus: 15% | Equity: 12,000 options
- L6 (Engineering Manager): Base $180,000 - $230,000 | Target Bonus: 20% | Equity: 25,000 options
- L7 (Senior Engineering Manager): Base $220,000 - $275,000 | Target Bonus: 25% | Equity: 45,000 options
- L8 (Director of Engineering): Base $270,000 - $340,000 | Target Bonus: 30% | Equity: 80,000 options
- L9 (Vice President of Engineering): Base $350,000 - $440,000 | Target Bonus: 40% | Equity: 150,000 options

## Section 2: Discretionary Merit Increase Guidelines
- Managers receive an allocated merit pool budget equal to 3.8% of aggregate team payroll.
- Out-of-cycle promotions and equity top-up grants require written approval from both the Department VP and Chief Financial Officer.
- Spot bonuses: Managers can award instant cash spot bonuses up to $1,500 without secondary approval via Workday Rewards.

## Section 3: Executive Retention Bonus Matrix
- Key leadership roles flagged for critical retention during strategic initiatives are eligible for a 12-month retention clawback bonus equal to 25% of annual base pay.
- Vesting of retention cash awards occurs semi-annually in two equal 50% tranches.
