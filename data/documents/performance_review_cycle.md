# Annual Performance Review and Promotion Guidelines

## Section 1: Review Cadence and Timelines
Acme Tech runs bi-annual performance and compensation review cycles.
- Summer Review Cycle: Launches June 1, self-evaluations due June 15, peer feedback due June 30, manager reviews finalized by July 15.
- Winter Review Cycle: Launches December 1, self-evaluations due December 15, manager reviews finalized by January 15.
- Compensation adjustments and promotions resulting from reviews take effect on February 1 and August 1 respectively.

## Section 2: Performance Rating Scale
Evaluations use a 5-tier calibrated rating distribution:
- Tier 1 (Needs Immediate Improvement): Below expectations; triggers an informal 30-day coaching plan.
- Tier 2 (Developing): Approaching full expectations in current level; targeted growth areas identified.
- Tier 3 (Consistently Meets Expectations): Solid, reliable delivery meeting all responsibilities of the role level (typically 60-70% of staff).
- Tier 4 (Frequently Exceeds Expectations): Substantial impact extending beyond direct project scope.
- Tier 5 (Exceptional Impact): Transformative contributions visible across the organization.

## Section 3: Promotion Criteria and Nomination
- Employees must spend a minimum of 12 months at their current level before being eligible for promotion nomination.
- Nominations require a promotion packet compiled in Lattice containing 3 peer reviews from cross-functional senior colleagues and approval from the Department Calibration Committee.
