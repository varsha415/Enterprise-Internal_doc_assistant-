# Project Falcon: Acquisition Strategy and Due Diligence Summary

> STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL & PRIVILEGED: High-level executive and management strategic memo. Project code: Falcon-9. Do not disclose under strict non-disclosure agreement penalties.

## Section 1: Acquisition Target and Transaction Terms
Acme Tech has entered exclusive definitive negotiations for the acquisition of CloudNova Inc.
- Target Company: CloudNova Inc (Specialists in distributed vector caching and edge AI runtime).
- Total Purchase Price: $42,000,000 USD (composed of 65% cash and 35% Acme Tech preferred stock).
- Transaction Code Name: "Falcon-9".
- Target Closing Date: November 15, 2026, subject to regulatory antitrust filings.

## Section 2: Key Personnel Retention and Golden Handcuff Pool
- CloudNova CEO and CTO will join Acme Tech as Senior Directors in the Emerging Technology division.
- A dedicated employee retention bonus pool of $5,500,000 has been earmarked for the top 18 core software engineers, vesting over 3 years in quarterly installments.
- Key talent non-compete covenants will span 24 months post-closing.

## Section 3: Technical Due Diligence Findings and Integration Roadmap
- CloudNova infrastructure currently runs on Kubernetes across AWS us-east-1 and eu-central-1.
- Technical debt audit revealed zero high-severity CVE vulnerabilities; 4 medium-priority dependencies require upgrade during post-merger integration.
- Acme Tech Core Infra team will initiate VPC peering within 14 days of deal closure.
