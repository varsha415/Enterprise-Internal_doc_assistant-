# Acme Tech Employee Handbook and Code of Conduct

## Section 1: Core Working Hours and Expectations
Acme Tech supports asynchronous collaboration while maintaining synchronized core collaboration windows. 
- All full-time employees are expected to be available for team syncs during core working hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in their local time zone.
- Standard weekly commitment is 40 hours for salaried employees.
- Team members must update their Slack status when stepping away for more than 45 minutes during core hours.

## Section 2: Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy
We believe sustainable performance requires genuine rest.
- Standard PTO allocation is 20 days per calendar year for full-time employees, accruing at 1.66 days per month.
- Employees can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Unused days beyond 5 expire on March 31.
- Sick leave is separate: Acme Tech provides 10 dedicated sick and mental health days annually, available from Day 1.
- Bereavement leave provides up to 5 consecutive paid business days for immediate family members.

## Section 3: Professional Development Stipend
Acme Tech invests directly in employee growth.
- Every full-time employee receives an annual Professional Development Stipend of $500 per calendar year.
- Eligible expenses include books, online courses, technical certifications (e.g., AWS, GCP, CKA), and conference tickets.
- Expenses must be submitted via Expensify under the "Learning & Development" category within 30 days of purchase.
- Approvals are handled automatically for certified vendors (Coursera, Udemy, O'Reilly).

## Section 4: Code of Business Conduct and Ethics
Acme Tech maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation.
- All employees must complete the Annual Integrity & Ethics Training within 30 days of hire and every October thereafter.
- Suspected violations should be reported to the People Ops hotline at ethics@acmetech.internal or anonymously via the Acme SpeakUp portal.
- Conflict of interest disclosure is mandatory for any secondary employment, advisory roles, or startup equity holdings exceeding 1%.
