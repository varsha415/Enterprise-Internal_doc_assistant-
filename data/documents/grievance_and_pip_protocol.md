# Manager Protocol for Performance Improvement Plans (PIP) and Grievances

> CONFIDENTIAL: Management and HR Business Partner (HRBP) operational procedure only.

## Section 1: Pre-PIP Coaching and Documentation Thresholds
Before placing an employee on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP):
- The manager must have held at least 3 documented 1-on-1 coaching sessions addressing specific performance gaps over a minimum 30-day period.
- Concrete, objective deliverables and timelines must be clearly established in writing.
- HRBP review and sign-off is mandatory before any PIP discussion is initiated with the employee.

## Section 2: Formal PIP Durations and Evaluation Checkpoints
- Standard PIP duration is 30 calendar days for individual contributors, and 60 calendar days for senior leads or people managers.
- Weekly formal status check-ins must be scheduled with both the manager and HRBP present.
- At the 15-day mark (or 30-day mark for 60-day plans), a formal Mid-Plan Assessment memo must be logged in Lattice.
- Outcomes: Successful completion (performance restored to Tier 3 standard), plan extension (maximum 14 days under exceptional circumstances), or immediate separation.

## Section 3: Workplace Grievance and Level 3 Disciplinary Actions
- Level 1: Documented verbal warning (remains on file for 6 months).
- Level 2: Written reprimand with mandatory corrective training (remains on file for 12 months).
- Level 3: Final written warning with potential temporary suspension without pay or immediate termination, requiring Chief Legal Officer notification.
