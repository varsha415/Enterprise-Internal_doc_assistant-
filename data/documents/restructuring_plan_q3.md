# Confidential Q3 Organizational Restructuring and Team Transition Plan

> STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL: For Department Heads and Managers Only. Contains Non-Public Organizational Strategy.

## Section 1: Strategic Realignment & Project Titan Sunset
Following strategic review by the Board of Directors, Acme Tech will reallocate capital from legacy incubation projects toward high-margin enterprise AI pipelines.
- Project Titan (Legacy Data Warehouse Migration) will officially sunset by end of Q3 2026.
- A 15% headcount redistribution will be enacted across affected engineering pods.
- High-performing engineers from Project Titan will be prioritized for transfer into Core Infrastructure and AI Operations pods.

## Section 2: Severance Packages and Separation Terms
For roles that cannot be absorbed into open requisitions:
- Standard severance package: 12 weeks of base salary continuation, plus 2 additional weeks per year of completed service.
- Full COBRA healthcare premium subsidy for 6 months.
- Immediate vesting acceleration for equity grants scheduled to vest within 90 days of separation date.
- Outplacement career advisory services provided through Lee Hecht Harrison for 90 days.

## Section 3: Manager Talking Points and Transition Schedule
- Notification Day (code-named Day Horizon) is tentatively set for October 14, 2026 at 09:00 AM Eastern.
- Managers must conduct 1-on-1 separation conversations in private conference rooms or secure video calls using HR Script Form B-2.
- IT access revocation will occur automatically at 01:00 PM on Notification Day to protect corporate intellectual property.
