# Management Overview of Equity Incentive Plan and Option Pool Allocation

> STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL: For hiring managers and compensation committees only.

## Section 1: 409A Valuation and Strike Price Benchmarks
Acme Tech's latest independent 409A common stock valuation was completed on December 15, 2025.
- Fair Market Value (FMV) / Current Exercise Strike Price: $3.40 per common share.
- Preferred Share Valuation (Series C round): $14.80 per share.
- Employee Option Pool Size: 1,500,000 unallocated shares authorized by the Board of Directors for FY2026.

## Section 2: Standard Vesting Schedules and Cliff Rules
- Default vesting schedule for all initial equity grants: 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff (25% vests at the 12-month anniversary of employment).
- Remaining 75% vests in equal monthly increments of 1/48th over the remaining 36 months.
- Exercise window upon voluntary departure: 90 days from termination date for vested incentive stock options (ISOs).
- Long-tenured employees (over 3 years of service) are eligible for an extended 5-year post-termination exercise window.

## Section 3: Manager Guidelines for Offering New-Hire Equity Grants
- Managers may offer up to the 75th percentile of the equity band without Board Compensation Committee pre-approval.
- Offers exceeding the 75th percentile must include a justification memo submitted to equity-admin@acmetech.internal.
