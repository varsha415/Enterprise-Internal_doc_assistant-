# Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Guidelines

## Section 1: Travel Booking and Approvals
All corporate business travel must be pre-approved by the direct manager before booking.
- Bookings must be made via the Acme Travel Portal (Navan / TripActions).
- Flights over 5 hours in duration are eligible for Premium Economy upgrades; domestic flights under 5 hours must be booked in standard Economy.
- Flight reservations should be completed at least 14 days in advance of departure to secure optimal corporate rates.

## Section 2: Hotel and Lodging Allowances
- The standard lodging cap is $250 per night (exclusive of local taxes) for domestic tier-2 cities.
- High-cost designated metropolitan areas (San Francisco, New York City, London, Tokyo) have an increased lodging cap of $350 per night.
- Airbnb and short-term rentals are permitted only for stays exceeding 5 consecutive nights and requiring approval from Finance.

## Section 3: Meal Per Diem and Ground Transportation
- Acme Tech provides a meal allowance of up to $75 per day for domestic travel ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- Alcohol purchases are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized business development dinner with clients.
- Ground transportation: Ride-share services (Uber, Lyft) or public transit are preferred. Rental cars require advance justification.

## Section 4: Receipt Policy and Submission Deadlines
- Itemized receipts are mandatory for any expense exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips without itemization will be rejected.
- Expense reports must be submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the travel end date. Late submissions beyond 60 days will not be reimbursed without CFO approval.
